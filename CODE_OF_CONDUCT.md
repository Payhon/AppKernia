# AppKernia Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want AppKernia to be a professional, welcoming place where people can learn, contribute, and disagree constructively, regardless of experience, background, identity, location, or preferred technology.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not a person's worth or motives.
- Be specific, patient, and generous when explaining a problem.
- Respect privacy, security embargoes, attribution, and third-party licenses.
- Accept correction and update claims when evidence changes.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused; move unrelated debate to an appropriate channel.
- Report platform, test, and device results honestly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets or personal data, sustained disruption, and knowingly falsified technical evidence are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not publish personal or sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Maintainers should handle reports consistently, minimize unnecessary disclosure, and avoid conflicts of interest. This code applies in repository spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
