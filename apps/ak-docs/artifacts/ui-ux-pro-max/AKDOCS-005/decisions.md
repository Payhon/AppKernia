# AKDOCS-005 decisions

## Voice

- Use first person only where the author explains why AppKernia exists.
- Elsewhere, address the developer through concrete benefits, workflows, and
  next actions.
- Remove public phrases such as local acceptance, runtime evidence, not a
  production claim, and delivery versus aspiration. Keep that information in
  internal implementation and screenshot records.
- Keep honest 0.x adoption guidance in the FAQ because it helps a developer
  choose responsibly; write it as practical guidance rather than a status report.

## Layout

- Use a continuous 1240 px content rail with 1 px vertical and horizontal rules.
- Use 12 columns on desktop: label in columns 1–3, headline and lead from
  column 4. Collapse to one column at 768 px.
- Replace floating rounded cards, gradient top rules, and page-level shadows
  with connected grids, neutral surfaces, and a single blue interaction accent.
- Retain rounded browser/phone frames and modest device depth inside the
  product slider because they represent the product surface, not the page chrome.

## Content structure

- Remove `HONEST MATURITY` in both locales and delete its unused CSS.
- Keep nine homepage content sections: origin, three surfaces, technology,
  features, capability matrix, source start, product tour, FAQ, and community.
- Preserve six feature links, nine technology items, and two manual sliders.
