- [ ] All visible copy uses `zh-CN` and `en-US` semantic keys with placeholder parity.
- [ ] Touch targets are at least 44 x 44 px and safe areas are respected.
- [ ] Search, filter, pagination, bookmark, share, logout, and destructive confirmations provide feedback.
- [ ] No emoji icons, hard-coded user-visible business copy, secret fixtures, or direct `up-*` usage.
- [ ] Android 360x800 screenshot captured.
- [ ] iOS 390x844 screenshot captured.
- [ ] Harmony 430x932 screenshot captured.
- [ ] Reduced motion, dynamic type, and English expansion reviewed.
- [ ] Static checks, contract tests, builds, and device smoke results are recorded truthfully.
